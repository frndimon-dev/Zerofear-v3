Courses / Basic Scooty Training

Basic Scooty Training

15-class structured programme for beginners.

Who is this for?

Complete beginners, people who cannot ride a bicycle, women starting from zero and riders who need a structured restart can enquire about this programme.

Class Structure

  1. Balance and body position
  2. Starting and stopping
  3. Brake and accelerator coordination
  4. Turning practice
  5. Slow-speed control
  6. Trainer-guided practical riding
  7. Road awareness
  8. Traffic progression when ready
  9. Helmet and safety practice
  10. Confidence building

Programme Format

15 classes. Approximately 30 minutes per class. Actual progression depends on learner ability, comfort and training conditions.

Bike Training

Learn clutch, gear, acceleration and coordination through structured practice.

What You Learn

  • Clutch control
  • Gear shifting
  • Acceleration coordination
  • Starting and stopping

A beginner-focused explanation.

অনেকেই ভাবেন cycle না জানলে scooty শেখা সম্ভব নয়। বাস্তবে learning-এর সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ বিষয় হলো balance, coordination এবং controlled practice।

Don't Rush Into Traffic

Learner-এর control এবং comfort তৈরি হওয়ার আগে busy traffic এ যাওয়ার প্রয়োজন নেই। Progression should be gradual.

Riding Tips / Beginner Guide

Common Beginner Riding Mistakes

Simple things that new riders should pay attention to.

Looking Down Too Much

Rider should maintain awareness of the road and surroundings, rather than continuously looking at the front wheel.

Overusing The Accelerator

Smooth control is generally more useful than sudden acceleration.

Turning Too Fast

Turning requires controlled speed, awareness and practice.

Riding Tips / Road Safety

What To Practice Before Traffic?

Control comes before complexity.

  • Starting and stopping smoothly
  • Brake control
  • Turning
  • Slow-speed balance
  • Awareness of surroundings
  • Basic road positioning

Traffic practice should be progressive and based on the learner's readiness.
